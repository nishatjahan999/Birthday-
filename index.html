01 · The School Corridor

A quiet school corridor in warm daylight. Fatin waits beside the classroom in his white shirt, black trousers and dark bag. A girl in a white hijab approaches him.
Girl: Hey, I want to tell you something.
Boy: Tell me.
Girl: First, take out your phone.
Boy: Okay, I’ve got my phone. Now tell me, what is it?
Girl: I created a website just for you.
Boy: For me? You did?
Girl: Yes. I’m not very experienced with these things, but I tried my best. I put everything I could into making it. I don’t know how much you’ll like it, but I really wanted to do something special for you.

She takes out her own phone and sends him the link on Messenger.

05 · The Notebook

She writes in a notebook, keeping the page hidden from the audience. Fatin watches quietly from another bench. She tears out four pages and places them into four separate envelopes, then sits beside him.

No dialogue. Only soft instrumental music and the quiet sounds of the classroom.

She gives him the envelopes one at a time. Tap each envelope to read its letter.

Envelope 01 · The Little Things

💌
Sometimes, the smallest things become the most beautiful memories without us even realizing it. A little conversation, a familiar smile, or a moment that seems ordinary to everyone else can mean so much more to someone. I don't know if you notice these little things, but some moments stay in my mind longer than they probably should. Maybe that's the beauty of certain people. They don't have to do anything extraordinary to make an ordinary day feel different. And somehow, some days become worth remembering simply because a certain person was there.

Envelope 02 · Things Left Unsaid

💌
Not every feeling finds its way into words, and not every thought needs to be spoken aloud. Sometimes, there are things we keep quietly to ourselves—not because they are unimportant, but because we don't know how to explain them. If you ever wonder why someone remembers the smallest details about you, perhaps those details were never really small to them. Some people leave little traces in our everyday lives without even knowing it. And sometimes, the things left unsaid are the ones that carry the most meaning.

Envelope 03 · A Day Worth Keeping

💌
I wanted today to be a little different from all the other days. Not because everything had to be perfect, but because some days deserve to be remembered in their own special way. Maybe you'll forget the little details someday—the cake, the letters, the rain, or the silly things we said. But I hope that whenever this day crosses your mind, you'll remember that someone took the time to make it meaningful for you. And perhaps, years from now, even the smallest memory from today will bring a quiet smile to your face. That would be enough for me.

Envelope 04 · Before You Go

💌
There is something I have wanted to say, but somehow, putting it into words has never been easy. I don't know what the future holds, or which memories will stay with us as time passes. Life has its own way of changing things, sometimes without warning. But I know that some moments are precious simply because they happened, regardless of what comes after them. So, if one day you happen to remember this little surprise, I hope you remember it with a smile. You don't have to find the perfect words to respond, and you don't have to promise anything. Just know that, for a little while, making you happy was something I truly wanted to do. And perhaps, that is all this moment ever needed to be.

06 · Thank You

He looks at her with a touched, grateful smile. She smiles shyly.
Boy: I honestly don't know what to say… Thank you for putting so much effort into making my birthday this special. I'll keep these letters close to my heart. You have no idea how much this means to me.

07 · Under One Umbrella

In gentle rain, Fatin holds a black umbrella over both of them as they walk side by side toward the school gate. She is a little shy. The school grounds and wet pavement are quiet.

Soft instrumental music, rain ambience, and a few quiet close-ups. No dialogue.

08 · One Last Letter

At the gate, she gives him a separate, longer letter. He holds the umbrella over them.
Girl: Don't open it yet. Read it after I leave, okay?
She gives him a hug. After a little while, she steps back.
Girl: Thank you for accepting all my gifts so beautifully. This special day wasn't just special for you… it was special for me, too.
Girl: Take care.

She walks away. Fatin watches until she is out of sight. Only then does he open the final letter.

For Fatin Almas ♡

I don't know when you'll read this, but I hope you'll read it slowly. You know, some memories are strange. We don't always realize we're making them until they become something we wish we could visit one more time. Maybe someday, we won't talk as often as we do now. Maybe life will take us to places we never imagined, and we'll become different versions of the people we are today. But I hope, somewhere in your memories, there will always be a little space for these ordinary days. The silly conversations, the random smiles, the moments that meant nothing to anyone else but somehow meant something to me. I wonder if you'll ever remember the little things I remember. Probably not all of them, and that's okay. Some memories are special even when only one person carries them. I never needed every moment to be perfect. I just wanted a few moments that would still feel beautiful when I looked back at them. And if you ever find yourself thinking about today, I hope you remember how someone tried to make you smile, how carefully every little thing was put together, and how much thought went into something that might have looked simple from the outside. Maybe I don't always know how to express what certain moments mean to me. So I put some of those feelings into these little things instead. Thank you for being a part of memories I'll probably keep for a long time. And if life ever makes these days feel far away, I hope you look back without sadness. I hope you smile, even if it's just for a second. Because some moments don't need to last forever to mean something forever. Take care of yourself, okay? And please, don't forget the little things. Sometimes, they're the ones that matter the most. ♡
